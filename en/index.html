Company Profile

COMPANY OVERVIEW · SINCE 1976

Half a century of bringing technology
to Korean industry

CHEMIKO supplies industrial materials, welding consumables, specialized welding equipment and automation systems to manufacturers in Korea. We work with leading manufacturers worldwide to help customers select the right materials and equipment for their production processes.

Our customers build ships, operate industrial plants, manufacture semiconductors and automobiles, and generate power, including nuclear energy. Each application has its own technical demands. We work with customers from the initial technical review through installation, operator training and maintenance.

PYEONGTAEK R&D CENTER

See the equipment in operation

At our Pyeongtaek R&D Center, customers can see equipment in operation, discuss a specific application with our technical team and receive hands-on training. The center also serves as our base for equipment maintenance and repair.

OUR BUSINESS

Materials, equipment and technical support

Industrial Materials & Welding Consumables

We supply specialty metals, nonferrous metals, welding consumables and brazing materials. Our recommendations take into account how a component is made and the conditions it must withstand in service.

Industrial Equipment & Automation

We supply precision welding systems, tube and pipe processing equipment, and automation solutions tailored to each customer’s production requirements.

Technical Support & Service

We demonstrate equipment, train operators and provide maintenance and repair services. When a technical issue arises, we work directly with the customer and the manufacturer to resolve it.

A Message from the CEO

Technology earns its place
on the production floor

Welcome to CHEMIKO.

When we consider a new technology, our first question is a practical one: What will it help our customer do better? That question has guided CHEMIKO since 1976. It shapes which manufacturers we work with, which products we introduce to Korea and how we support the people who use them.

Some of our most important work takes place before an order is placed. We listen to the engineers and operators who know the process, review the technical requirements with the manufacturer and explain why we believe a particular material or machine is the right choice. A sound recommendation should be one our customer can understand and rely on.

Our responsibility continues after delivery. If a machine does not perform as expected, our role is to understand what is happening on site, bring the right people into the discussion and follow the issue through to resolution. To me, that is what it means to be a technical partner.

As manufacturing evolves, we will continue to seek out technologies that offer a practical advantage on the production floor. We will bring the same care to introducing them that we bring to supporting the equipment our customers already rely on.

Thank you for your trust in CHEMIKO.

CHEMIKO Co., Ltd.

President & CEOSoo Jin Ha

Privacy Policy

CHEMIKO CO., LTD. treats users’ personal data with care and complies with the relevant laws.

1. Personal data collected and method of collection

CHEMIKO collects the items below through the document and quote request form on this website.

When collectedOn submission of the document and quote request form
Required itemsCompany name, contact name, email address, enquiry details
Optional itemsContact details, partner requested, type of item or document requested

We collect only the information that users enter themselves, and do not collect personal data by any other means.

This website does not use cookies or analytics tools for advertising identification or user behaviour analysis.

2. Purpose of using personal data

  • Provision of the requested product documents, technical data sheets and quotations
  • Checking and replying to enquiries
  • Technical consultation and follow-up contact relating to the request

Personal data collected is not used for any purpose other than those above. If the purpose changes, consent is obtained in advance.

3. Retention and use period of personal data

It is destroyed without delay once the purpose of use has been achieved. However, for enquiry record management and follow-up consultation, from the date of receipt it is kept for three years and then destroyed.

Information subject to a statutory retention obligation is kept for the period set by the relevant law.

4. Outsourcing of personal data processing and transfer abroad

CHEMIKO outsources the work below for website operation and enquiry handling, and the servers of the company concerned are located outside Korea.

ContractorOutsourced workItems transferredRetention period

GitHub, Inc.Website hostingAccess logs generated during connectionIn accordance with that company’s policy

If the contractor changes, notice will be given through this policy.

5. Provision of personal data to third parties

CHEMIKO does not provide users’ personal data to third parties, with the following exceptions.

  • Where the user has given prior consent
  • Where an investigative authority makes a lawful request in accordance with the law

Where requested material has to be obtained from an overseas manufacturer, we give notice of that fact and of the scope of provision, and forward the material once consent has been obtained.

6. Rights of the data subject and how to exercise them

Users may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of their personal data, or the suspension of its processing.

Requests made to the contact below in writing, by telephone or by email are handled without delay. A request may also be made through a representative, in which case a letter of authorisation must be submitted.

If you request deletion of your personal data, it is destroyed immediately and we reply so that you can confirm this.

7. Procedure and method for destroying personal data

  • Electronic files: permanently deleted by a method that prevents recovery.
  • Paper documents: shredded or incinerated.

Personal data is destroyed within five days of the date on which the reason for keeping it ends, whether because the retention period has expired or the purpose of processing has been achieved.

8. Measures to ensure the security of personal data

  • The staff who handle personal data are limited to the minimum number required for the work.
  • Material containing personal data is held in an area with controlled access rights.
  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS) is applied to the website and to all submission paths.

Main industries

Half a century of experience, with advanced technology
Building the future of our industry together.

With experience built since 1976 and global technical cooperation, we strengthen our customers’ production competitiveness from material supply to process automation.

CHEMIKO specialises in the supply of special industrial materials, welding and processing equipment, and automation solutions. Drawing on experience built in shipbuilding, plant, automotive, semiconductor, nuclear and energy, we propose products and technology suited to each customer’s processes and requirements.

Through close cooperation with specialist manufacturers in Germany, France, Italy and Finland, we bring advanced technology to Korean production sites. From equipment selection to process application and technical support, we work between customer and manufacturer to find practical answers.

Building on the trust and expertise held for half a century, CHEMIKO is broadening the scope of its automation solutions and working with customers towards better production environments and continued growth.
